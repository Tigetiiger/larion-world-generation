Unofficial, AI-assisted port of Larion 4.3.1 (upstream `main`) to Minecraft 26.2.

- **General**
  - Updated to Minecraft 26.2 (Java 25). Fabric only; NeoForge and Forge builds were dropped.
  - No longer requires Fabric API.
  - Server-side only on multiplayer servers.
- **World generation**
  - Terrain is unchanged from Larion 4.3.1 on 1.21.1: same seed, same landscape.
  - Adapted to Minecraft's new way of finding the preliminary surface (`preliminary_surface_level`), reproducing the old behavior exactly.
  - Added the new sulfur caves surface rules, so sulfur caves get their sulfur and cinnabar blocks.
  - Updated the overworld dimension settings to the 26.2 format (keeps the -128 to 512 world height).
  - Updated the 2D spaghetti cave function to the 26.2 format (Larion's own caves are unaffected).
